# RM1 P6-1 observability formal-granularity-binding design admission

## 1. Authority and problem

Dexter explicitly made the formal granularity-gate binding a substantive new DESIGN scope for P6-1
observability.  P6-1 needs public authentication/recovery diagnostics and remote verification liveness that
do not disclose secrets; the confirmed 500 S01/C01 discovery and accepted observability standard require a
real implementation-facing design before any code.  The agent-discovered edge-root projection defect is a
separate input whose owner/root/ref closure must be proved rather than hidden in an edge-local patch.

This CP-U11 interlude creates a new, explicitly named `formal-granularity-binding` design cycle.  It is not
a continuation, rename or rewrite of CP-U10 or either older planned review cycle.

## 2. Strict document-only scope

The exact files permitted are the fourteen paths in `rm1p6-cp-u11-control-plane-manifest.json`.  They are
limited to a future author design, formal binding manifest, new-cycle independent review inputs/verdicts,
author intake, future extension manifest/source disposition and CP evidence.  The future formal binding must
reopen business sources, the observability decision, full 500 S01/C01 denominator, edge-root owning/ref
sources, six-dimensional routed memory and the standards matrix.  It must bind exact sources and paths,
not merely describe them.

No runner, foundation, backend, OpenAPI, generated, migration, frontend or dynamic implementation path is
authorized.  Nor may this package claim business or dynamic observability PASS.
