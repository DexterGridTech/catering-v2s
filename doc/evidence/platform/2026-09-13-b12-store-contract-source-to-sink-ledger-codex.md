## Closure notes

- `TD-01` and `TD-02` explicitly account for command and task-read derived-status call sites. `TD-02` includes both single and batch variants and records the empty-list no-JDBC branch; this is not a silent omission.
- `TD-04`, `TD-16` and `TD-17` show legal persistence method reuse with every caller variant. The post-relocation physical sink count is therefore lower than the pre-write execution-point count without losing a point.
- No row uses a wildcard or method-name-only locator. No persistence public boundary accepts raw SQL, SQL fragments, wrappers, mappers or generic executor arguments. The final focused run is compilation/module-test proof only (`business=NOT_APPLICABLE`); this ledger does not upgrade it to backend acceptance.
- The 39 keys are the static source-to-sink ledger. Effective SQL equivalence, transaction/proxy behavior, lock/CAS, idempotency, rollback, audit and authoritative readback remain separately reported at their required evidence tier.
